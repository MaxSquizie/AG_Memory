Return exactly one CHOICE and nothing else. POSSESSION means the proposition says that the already identified SUBJECT possesses, has, holds as an available belonging/resource, or is the possessor of OBJECT; existential possessive wording counts when it expresses the same relation. OTHER_RELATION means SUBJECT and OBJECT participate in an action, location, comparison, transfer, perception, class/property statement, or any other relation instead. UNCLEAR means the source does not safely determine which reading applies. Judge the semantic relation in the whole sentence, not the surface verb alone.
