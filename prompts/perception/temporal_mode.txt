Return exactly STATE, EVENT, PROCESS, or AMBIGUOUS. Classify only the stated predicate occurrence in its temporal frame. Do not infer a global property of the predicate. Stable knowledge, preference, possession, status, or condition is STATE even when expressed by an imperfective verb. PROCESS requires the source to present internal activity or development across the interval. Use AMBIGUOUS only when the source genuinely permits multiple readings, not merely because the predicate is imperfective.
