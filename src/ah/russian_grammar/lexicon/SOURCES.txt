infinitive_control.tsv describes selected lexical frames applicable in a
construction with a dependent infinitive. Fields: embedded scope, source of
the infinitive's subject, possible case of the matrix recipient, verb lemmas.
These are project annotations. The GrammarEngine flag licenses an infinitive
but does not identify its subject. Background: Academy Grammar, connections
of the verb with the infinitive, and Timberlake, A Reference Grammar of
Russian, table 4.13:
https://slovari.ru/default.aspx?0a0=1771&p=5315&s=0
https://mercaba.org/SANLUIS/IDIOMAS/Ruso/Reference%20grammar%20russian.pdf

bare_government.tsv lists licensed bare oblique cases by infinitive. The
importer reads the two GrammarEngine infinitive files at the revision and
SHA256 values given for infinitives.tsv above (MIT). It reads explicit case
lists and entry declarations, including stress-marked macros. A perfective
macro with omitted case list inherits from its named imperfective partner
as defined in rus_infinitives.inc; an explicit INTR suppresses inheritance.
Russian case labels are mapped to datv, ablt, gent and gen2. Cases are
pooled across a lemma's senses. A row expresses possibilities only.
The table also includes the case slots of the project annotations below.

bare_roles.tsv contains project-authored readings for selected constructions.
Each row specifies a governor, the dependent's case and a possible semantic
role. The genitive of quantity can realize the direct object of a transfer.
A writing verb's instrumental allows several readings, including instrument
and manner. A case listed by GrammarEngine alone supplies no role label.
For comparison, FrameBank distinguishes participant roles by construction and
groups examples by head, form and semantics of the dependent. Reference:
https://github.com/olesar/framebank/tree/e60da2a38f645db21c55aa0e7ff9730e92e7e2ca
FrameBank data are separately available there under GPL-3.0.

The spatial slot table uses exact place, destination and source role labels
with corresponding case formulas. Nominals are inferred from construction
titles containing a single preposition and a locally matching inanimate noun.
The noun evidence retains the preposition and direction. Geographic names
also use OpenCorpora's Geox feature. Both require a licensed governing verb.
An adverb has a corpus reading only when it is the sole candidate in at least
two construction titles for one role. These attestations pool senses and do
not provide an exhaustive ontology or word sense disambiguation.
adjunct_readings.tsv contains the project's supplemental lexical annotations
for calendar expressions, nonspatial prepositional expressions and adverbs.

required_arguments.tsv is project-authored. Keys with * name a construction
and its required immediate roles. Repeated rows are conjunctive, roles in a
row are alternatives. The lexical entries restrict assertion of the isolated
placement reading to an overt or shared patient. FrameBank constructions
4193 and 4194 attest the accusative patient; the conservative admission
policy is ours. The table is not an exhaustive Russian valency dictionary.
