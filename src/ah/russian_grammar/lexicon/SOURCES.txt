case annotations; "-" means the sources supplied no mapped case. It combines:

1. The complete preposition section of GrammarEngine rus_prepositions.sol
   (postpositions form a separate category). Copyright Ilya Koziev.

infinitive_control.tsv describes selected lexical frames applicable in a
construction with a dependent infinitive. Fields: embedded scope, source of
the infinitive's subject, possible case of the matrix recipient, verb lemmas.
These are project annotations. The GrammarEngine flag licenses an infinitive
but does not identify its subject. Background: Academy Grammar, connections
of the verb with the infinitive, and Timberlake, A Reference Grammar of
Russian, table 4.13:
https://slovari.ru/default.aspx?0a0=1771&p=5315&s=0
https://mercaba.org/SANLUIS/IDIOMAS/Ruso/Reference%20grammar%20russian.pdf

clause-connectives.tsv lists observed connective forms, their dependency
relation, and governor lemmas for selected clausal complements. Fixed
expressions are reconstructed from contiguous tokens with the UD fixed
relation. These are positive attestations rather than a complete valency
dictionary. Derived from the Russian Universal Dependencies treebanks:
- Russian GSD training set (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://universaldependencies.org/treebanks/ru_gsd/
