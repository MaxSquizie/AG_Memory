## Belyaev literary monolith

`05_belyaev_amphibian_monolith.md` is an unadapted excerpt from Alexander R. Belyaev, *Человек-амфибия* (1928), chapter `Покинутая «Медуза»`. The text was taken from Russian Wikisource, where the work is marked as public domain in Russia and life+70 jurisdictions.

The test intentionally keeps the literary wording intact; only whitespace/typographic spacing is normalized for the local UTF-8 fixture.
