# Slice 12.4 — relative clauses and compound connectives

- Relative-clause marker tokens are clause operators, not actants.
- Relative antecedents are rebound into child predicate roles; morphology resolves the role when unique, otherwise a tiny finite `relative_role` probe may abstain.
- Multi-word clause connectives such as temporal/cause/purpose forms are recognized as one connector span and excluded from actant candidates.
- Omitted subjects in conservative adverbial child clauses can inherit a unique parent subject.
- Added deterministic `читать/прочитать -> read` predicate mapping.
- Repeated identical open predicate-symbol outputs are collapsed as format-only repetition; different outputs remain invalid.
